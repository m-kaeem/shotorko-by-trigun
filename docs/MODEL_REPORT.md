# Shotorko model report: data, accuracy and robustness

This report supplements [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). The figures are regenerated by `python -m src.train` into `artifacts/metrics.json` and shown live on the app's *Model trust* page.

## Robustness to incomplete data

Field data is often partial. LightGBM handles missing values natively, but only for patterns it saw during training. A second, **robust** model per horizon (point + P10/P90) is therefore trained on the train + validation rows plus three degraded copies. In each copy, whole feature groups are blanked at random (humidity 35%, temperature 25%, max/min 25%, cases 35%, ward profile 30%, rainfall 10%), and 35% of rows have their history cut to 1–12 weeks. Ten completeness scenarios are then measured on the test years (`src/robust.py`). Examples: with complete data the robust model matches the standard one (MAE 1.01 vs 1.04 at 3 weeks). For a new area with only rain and temperature for 8 weeks it reaches 1.11, against 1.83 for the standard model and 1.30 for a no-data seasonal average. The API picks the robust model automatically when inputs have gaps. Interval coverage under these scenarios is 74–84%.

## Data distribution across splits

The test years are quieter than validation: 2.25 vs 3.20 mean cases per ward-week, and 6.8% vs 13.6% of weeks above the alert threshold. Weather means are similar (rain 32.6 vs 39.2 mm/week, 26.7 vs 26.9 °C, humidity 71% in both). This explains why test MAE is lower than validation MAE while alert precision is lower. Train-only model R²: 0.87 on train (in-sample), 0.73 on validation and 0.70 on test, so there is a modest, expected generalisation gap.

## Hospital readiness

When a ward is High or Critical, the panel lists the city's dengue-treating public hospitals (DGHS notices and DGHS/Civil Surgeon admission reports; `src/hospitals.py`). It shows the city's expected dengue load for the forecast week as the summed ward forecasts against the summed seasonal normal, plus preparedness actions and the DGHS health line 16263. No open real-time bed feed exists, so pressure is forecast-based. A DGHS hospital admission feed is the natural Stage-2 integration.
